HOW SPECLOCK WORKS

From draft to active terms

01

Publish and accept

The publisher freezes the first version and names a counterparty. That wallet accepts the revision.

02

Commit and reveal

A third wallet commits a fingerprint of its challenge. When the reveal window opens, the same wallet submits the evidence and salt.

03

Evaluate

Validators assess both readings of the same scenario. Confirmed ambiguity blocks the revision. An inconclusive challenge stays open for another evaluation.

04

Revise or activate

The publisher can propose corrected wording. An accepted latest revision can activate after review closes if there are no unresolved challenges or confirmed blockers.

Using Studionet

Public reads work without a wallet. To sign a write, connect an EIP-1193 wallet on chain 61999. On mobile, open the app in your wallet’s browser if your regular browser does not expose a wallet. Credits are records in the contract.
